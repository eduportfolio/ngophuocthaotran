# Assets / Images Guide
Place your photos in this folder with the exact filenames below.

## Profile
- `profile.jpg` — Thảo Trân portrait photo (used in About section)

## Honors & Awards
- `award-1i1w.jpg` — Photo from 1I1W Gold Medal / Neem shampoo research
- `award-olympiad.jpg` — Biology Olympiad certificate or ceremony photo
- `award-wsc.jpg` — World Scholar's Cup photo (team or medal)

## Extracurricular Activities
- `activity-choray.jpg` — Volunteer work at Chợ Rẫy Hospital
- `activity-aiLinh.jpg` — Ái Linh volunteer activities
- `activity-biospaze.jpg` — BioSpaZe research club photo

## Tips
- Recommended dimensions: 1200×800px (landscape) for activity/award photos
- Portrait photo: 600×800px minimum
- Format: JPG or WEBP preferred
- If a photo is missing, the site shows a styled placeholder automatically
